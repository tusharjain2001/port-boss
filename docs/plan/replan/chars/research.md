Research for the Port Boss cast. Searched 2026-10-31. Every claim links its source. Where no developer said something, the line is marked **(our read)**: that's an inference from looking at the games, not a quote.

General readability practice: a character should "remain readable in grayscale, at a distance, and in motion", checked with a blackout test and a three-read order of silhouette, then colour blocks, then detail ([RocketBrush](https://rocketbrush.com/blog/shape-language-in-game-character-design-how-to-make-characters-readable-and-consistent), [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/character-design-shape-language-and-readability/)). Third-person players mostly see the character's **back**.

## What we take for Port Boss

1. **Bigger head, bigger hands, bigger boots.** Every lovable cast above has a head well over the realistic 1/7 of the height: Fall Guys, R.E.P.O., PEAK and Overcooked. Our current worker is about 7 heads tall with a small head, so its face can't be read at play distance. **Target: about 4.5 heads tall**, hands about 1.3× and boots about 1.4× realistic size. Carrying crates is our core verb, so hands and boots are where the eye goes.
2. **Body type is the silhouette.** Sea of Thieves shows that fat, thin, old and young bodies make one animation set look like six characters. Our 6 bases differ in mass and height first, outfit second.
3. **Headwear is the #1 identity slot.** It's top of the silhouette, seen from the third-person camera from behind and above, and it's the reward every game uses (Crashout Crew, PEAK, R.E.P.O., Lethal Company mods). So every preset has a *different* headwear shape: beanie, tall hard hat, sou'wester, bandana, flat cap and diving helmet.
4. **The player colour is a separate fixed channel, not the outfit.** Lethal Company players fall back on suit colour, and Overcooked 2 added player markers. With free customisation two players can end up dressed the same, so the **slot colour** (orange, blue, green, red, as in the current HUD) goes on parts nobody can recolour: an **armband**, a **back patch** (we mostly see backs), the **name tag** over the head and the **hard-hat stripe**. Outfit colours never use those four saturated hues at full strength.
5. **Faces: big eyes, a few blendable expressions.** R.E.P.O. gets all its emotion from two eyes and 6 expressions that mix into 21. We do the same: eyes and brows as blend shapes (happy, strain, panic, smug, plus blink), driven by game events (lifting = strain, falling = panic, sale = happy, Marlowe = smug or angry).
6. **Comedy comes from the body, not jokes.** Fall Guys built the body to pose, and Human: Fall Flat's comedy is its controls. Ours has to come from weight: stocky waddles, lanky flails, round rolls and wiry speed-walks. One skeleton, but **per-preset animation tweaks** (stride, arm swing, idle, fall reaction) give each a personality.
7. **A generator beats sliders for first-time players.** Sea of Thieves' Infinite Pirate Generator shows 8 random pirates to pick from. We keep the existing clipboard editor for tinkerers but open on **6 presets plus Randomise**.
