# Reference research for the 6 art directions (checked 2026-10-31)

Steam figures were read from each store page's embedded review data (all languages, all purchase types). They are shown as "X% of N". Awards come from Wikipedia unless another source is named. "Nominated" means the game did not win. Every link returned HTTP 200 on 2026-10-31.

## 1. Golden Hour Adventure (premium stylised realism)
| Ref | Year | Link | Why it counts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sea of Thieves | 2018 (Steam 2020) | https://store.steampowered.com/app/1172620/ | 89% of 374,495. Won Visual Design at the TIGA Awards 2018. Won BAFTA Evolving Game 2021 |
| Tchia | 2023 (Steam 2024) | https://store.steampowered.com/app/1496590/ | 87% of 925. Won TGA 2023 Games for Impact |
| Sword of the Sea | 2025 | https://store.steampowered.com/app/2453160/ | 90% of 1,791. Directed by Matt Nava, art director of Journey. Nominated for Golden Joystick Best Visual Design |
| Kena: Bridge of Spirits | 2021 (Steam 2022) | https://store.steampowered.com/app/1954200/ | 92% of 17,757. Won TGA 2021 Best Independent Game and Best Debut Indie. Nominated for Best Art Direction |
| Journey | 2012 (PC 2019) | https://store.steampowered.com/app/638230/ | 94% of 38,892. Won DICE Art Direction, BAFTA Artistic Achievement and GDCA Best Visual Arts |

How it's made: [SIGGRAPH 2018 "The Technical Art of Sea of Thieves" (PDF)](https://history.siggraph.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/2018-Talks-Ang_The-Technical-Art-of-Sea-of-Thieves.pdf) and [Valentine Kozin, GDC 2019, SoT tech art (Vimeo)](https://vimeo.com/326413164).
