# Research: the hits (design, structure, virality, EA)

Scope: team size, story, objectives, session length, co-op/voice, progression, virality, Early Access. Reviews, prices, dates, CCU and sales are out of scope (they come from the Steam pull), and any that appear below are only there as context for a source. "Unverified" means I could not confirm it on a live page. Where a fact comes from a search-engine excerpt and not a fetched page, the bullet says "(search excerpt)".

---

## R.E.P.O. (semiwork)
- **Team:** "Small team based in Uppsala, Sweden." The exact headcount is unverified: semiwork has published none, and a company directory lists 7 employees (low reliability). https://www.newsweek.com/entertainment/video-games/what-repo-viral-horror-video-game-explained-2046020 ; https://funnelfeedr.com/fi/company/se/semiwork-studios-ab-5591704589 (search excerpt) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Story:** Light framing. You work "under the employ of a mysterious computer intelligence" to extract valuables "from the haunted remains of a long lost humanity". The goal is to "make money for The Taxman". Steam promises "more story elements" for 1.0. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/ ; https://www.destructoid.com/repo-tips-to-make-money/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Objective:** Each level has a quota per extraction point, and you "cannot escape the level until you do so". Layouts are randomized roguelike levels. Failing a quota is reported to send you to "Disposal" and delete the save (search excerpt, unverified on a fetched page). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R.E.P.O. ; https://www.destructoid.com/repo-tips-to-make-money/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Session/run:** Per-level length is unverified.
- **Co-op/voice:** Up to 6 players, with proximity voice chat. Steam lists "physics, proximity voice chat and scary monsters". https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Progression:** Between levels you spend SURPLUS at the "Service Station" on permanent upgrades, weapons and tools. Meta-progression and player customization were promised for 1.0, and the 0.4.0 update (7 May 2026) added cosmetics. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R.E.P.O. ; https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Virality:** The game got little attention for about a week. Then Twitch and YouTube streams were cut into clips for TikTok, X and YouTube Shorts, and "exaggerated physics simulation" made the moments shareable. The best-documented meme is "Repo I Love": Twitch streamer PresidentLloyd's clip of player Just_CSmallz singing "Chest Pain (I Love)" was posted 20 Mar 2025. A TikTok repost got 5.5M+ views, and TikTok animations got 17.9M and 28.1M views. https://www.newsweek.com/entertainment/video-games/what-repo-viral-horror-video-game-explained-2046020 ; https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/repo-i-love (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **EA:** Yes. EA launched with "4 levels, 29 enemies, many items, weapons and upgrades". The planned EA length was "around a year and a half". There is no public dated roadmap, only a 1.0 wishlist. The developers have said "future updates won't take so long". https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/ ; https://www.gamesradar.com/games/horror/one-day-we-were-crossing-our-fingers-for-rent-money-and-the-next-we-had-millions-of-players-repo-devs-get-candid-say-the-game-literally-made-semiwork-as-a-studio-survive-and-insist-future-updates-wont-take-so-long/ (accessed 2026-10-31)

## Lethal Company (Zeekerss)
- **Team:** Solo developer (Zeekerss, a former Roblox creator). https://naavik.co/digest/lethal-company-takeaways/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeekerss (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Story:** Light framing. You are a contracted worker for "the Company", and the lore draws on SCP. Journal entries from a previous crew add environmental flavour. https://store.steampowered.com/app/1966720/Lethal_Company/ ; https://naavik.co/digest/lethal-company-takeaways/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Objective:** Meet a profit quota at the end of each 3-day cycle. The quota rises every cycle, and missing it ends the run. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethal_Company (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Session/run:** One in-game day is about 11 minutes of real time, and the ship leaves automatically at midnight. https://sg.news.yahoo.com/lethal-companys-co-op-horror-182857583.html (search excerpt); https://orbispatches.com/gaming-faq/how-long-is-a-day-in-lethal-company (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Co-op/voice:** Up to 4 players, with proximity voice chat and proximity text chat. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethal_Company (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Progression:** Credits buy equipment, ship furniture and access to riskier, more lucrative moons. Progress resets when a run fails. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethal_Company (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Virality:** The first cited TikTok, by @imdaukey on 24 Oct 2023 (the day after launch), got 977K plays. A 16 Nov 2023 TikTok by @spitz_official ("first time seeing a Forest Keeper") got 10M+ plays in a week. Growth was "mostly boosted by streamers from all over the globe", but no named streamer is verified. https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/lethal-company ; https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/editorial-lethal-company-the-indie (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **EA:** Yes, from 23 Oct 2023. EA launched with "at least 7 planets... 9 creatures... 8 tools". The developer planned "to finish it within 6 months" and wanted later updates to be "bigger and less frequent". There is no public roadmap. Updates continued irregularly: a burnout break in Sep 2024, v70 in Jun 2025 and v73 in Oct 2025. https://store.steampowered.com/app/1966720/Lethal_Company/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethal_Company ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeekerss ; https://lethal-company.fandom.com/wiki/Version_73 (search excerpt) (accessed 2026-10-31)

## Schedule I (TVGS)
- **Team:** Tyler (Sydney) is the lead designer, programmer and artist. Only 3 people are credited: Tyler, plus music by KAESUL and graffiti by Cody To. A Jan 2026 report says he has since hired one more person (Russian-language source). https://sportskeeda.com/esports/who-created-schedule-1-so-popular ; https://shazoo.ru/2026/01/07/177866/sozdatel-schedule-1-bolse-ne-solo-razrabotcik-on-nanial-eshhe-odnogo-celoveka-i-otkryl-polnocennyi-ofis (search excerpt) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Story:** A real narrative device. Uncle Nelson guides you by payphone after he is arrested, and a rival cartel (the Benzies, led by Thomas) blows up your RV and later offers a truce. A final mission is to "wipe them out". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schedule_I_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Objective:** Open-world growth from "small-time dope pusher to kingpin". You unlock regions, customers and properties, and an end mission opens once every region is unlocked. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3164500/Schedule_I/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schedule_I_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Session/run:** Unverified.
- **Co-op/voice:** A maximum of 4 players, in both the demo and the full game. The host owns the save. In-game voice chat is unverified: a forum post says it is "in the roadmap". https://insider-gaming.com/schedule-1-multiplayer-how-does-it-work/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Progression:** Hire dealers, cooks, janitors and botanists (automation via staff), buy properties and front businesses, and mix recipes. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schedule_I_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Virality:** "Gained popularity from being livestreamed on Twitch and TikTok." A quiet beta in Dec 2024 built word of mouth on Steam and Reddit before prominent streamers gave it "its initial push". The co-op demo was free. No named streamer is verified. Twitch viewership peaked at 225.4K on 1 Apr 2025. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schedule_I_(video_game) ; https://sportskeeda.com/esports/who-created-schedule-1-so-popular ; https://streamscharts.com/games/schedule-i (search excerpt) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **EA:** Yes, from 24 Mar 2025. Steam promises "substantial content updates on a monthly basis" over about 2 years of EA, with 1.0 planned for Q4 2027. The roadmap is a public Trello board with no dates. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3164500/Schedule_I/ ; https://mein-mmo.de/schedule-1-roadmap-trello-infos/ (search excerpt) (accessed 2026-10-31)

## Content Warning (Landfall; Wilnyl, Philip, thePetHen, Skog, Zorro)
- **Team:** 5 developers. It was built Feb–Apr 2024, mostly at an internal Landfall game jam in Seoul. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_Warning_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Story:** Light framing. You film monsters in "the Old World" to go viral on "SpöökTube". https://store.steampowered.com/app/2881650/Content_Warning/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Objective:** Hit a views quota within 3 days, one dive per day. Failing wipes your money and gear and resets the quota. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_Warning_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Session/run:** A run is 3 in-game days (3 dives). Minutes per dive are unverified. https://store.steampowered.com/app/2881650/Content_Warning/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Co-op/voice:** 2–4 players. Steam lists "Voice chat". That it is proximity-based comes only from a third-party vendor page (weak source). https://store.steampowered.com/app/2881650/Content_Warning/ ; https://voxbooster.com/blog/voice-changer-content-warning (search excerpt) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Progression:** Ad revenue buys gear within a run. Sponsor deals pay Meta Coins, a persistent currency for Sky Island customization and hats. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_Warning_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Virality:** First spark: the game launched free for the first 24 hours on 1 Apr 2024, Landfall's April Fools tradition. About 6.2M users claimed it, and concurrent players hit 50K within 12 hours. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_Warning_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **EA:** No, it launched as a full release. Post-launch updates continued (monster roster to 32 by May 2024, lobby codes in Nov 2025, console ports in Apr 2026). No roadmap was found. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_Warning_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)

## PEAK (Aggro Crab + Landfall)
- **Team:** 7 at the jam (3 from Aggro Crab, 4 from Landfall). They worked in a Seoul Airbnb for about a month in Feb 2025, then each studio finished development in its own office. https://www.tweaktown.com/news/106032/comedy-climber-peak-was-conceived-in-a-hot-tub-and-developed-in-an-airbnb/index.html (search excerpt) ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak_(video_game) ; https://www.actugaming.net/peak-le-jeu-phenomene-qui-sest-vendu-a-4-5-millions-dexemplaires-737059/ (accessed 2026-10-31)

- **Objective:** A run-based climb through 6 biomes to the summit, with harder difficulties unlocked after. The map rotates every 24 hours. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak_(video_game) ; https://store.steampowered.com/app/3527290/PEAK/ (accessed 2026-10-31)

- **Co-op/voice:** 4 players, with proximity chat. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3527290/PEAK/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Progression:** Badges ("Accolades") unlock cosmetics, and there is no gameplay power meta. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3527290/PEAK/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)

Hype spread across TikTok and YouTube, the $5–8 price is credited as a key driver, and a sarcastic developer post about outselling their previous game drew attention. No named streamer is verified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak_(video_game) ; https://www.actugaming.net/peak-le-jeu-phenomene-qui-sest-vendu-a-4-5-millions-dexemplaires-737059/ ; https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/from-game-jam-to-10-million-units-indie-hit-peak-success (accessed 2026-10-31)

It has had free biome updates (Mesa Aug 2025, Roots Nov 2025, Final Ascent Aug 2026). https://www.inverse.com/gaming/peak-steam-landcrab-friendslop-update-interview-nick-kaman-pc ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)

## Cross-game patterns (only what the sources above support)
1. **Tiny teams.** Lethal Company, TCG Card Shop and Schedule I (core) were built by one person, Supermarket Simulator by one developer by his own account, Content Warning by 5, PEAK by 7, and PowerWash by 11. Only DRAGO (studio of about 50) is clearly larger.
2. **Story is thin or a framing device in 11 of 13.** A faceless quota-giver (the Company, the Taxman/"computer intelligence") or a premise line (plane crash, Hell, SpöökTube, abandoned gas station) is enough. Only Schedule I (Uncle Nelson, the Benzies), Bellwright (framed exile) and PowerWash (tycoon and volcano) carry a real plot.
3. **Quotas on a clock are the shared co-op loop.** Lethal Company (3-day quota, ship leaves at midnight, about 11-minute days), Content Warning (3-day views quota) and R.E.P.O. (per-extraction quotas) all put a deadline on failure, and failing ends or resets the run.
4. **4 players is the norm.** It is the cap in Lethal Company, Content Warning, PEAK, Chained Together, Liar's Bar, Schedule I, Supermarket (co-op) and Bellwright. R.E.P.O. (6) and WEBFISHING (12) go higher.
5. **Proximity voice is confirmed in the three biggest "friendslop" co-op hits (Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., PEAK), but it is not universal.** WEBFISHING is text-only, and the three sims are single-player or added co-op later.
6. **Physics and failure comedy is what spreads.** R.E.P.O.'s "exaggerated physics" was cited as shareable, Chained Together's fail clips ("Drunk Ninja"), Liar's Bar's neck-stretch bug (22M views) and WEBFISHING's launch trickshots all went round. Each one is a short clip of something going wrong.
7. **Streamers then clips, not paid marketing.** The pattern was a quiet launch, then streamers, then clips on TikTok, X and Shorts (R.E.P.O. about a week; Lethal Company "a few weeks"; Supermarket and TCG streamers picked it up "without being contacted"). Named first sparks were Alanzoka (Liar's Bar, day 2), DisguisedToast and LilyPichu (Chained Together, day 1), and @imdaukey on TikTok (Lethal Company, day 1).
8. **Free access as an ignition tool.** Content Warning (free for 24 hours), Gas Station Simulator (Next Fest-era demo plus a free prologue), Schedule I (free co-op demo), PowerWash (free itch.io demo), Bellwright (Next Fest demo) and WEBFISHING (free jam build) all used it.
9. **EA was common but not required.** 8 of 13 launched in EA (R.E.P.O., Lethal Company, PowerWash, Supermarket, TCG, Schedule I, Bellwright, Liar's Bar). The other 5 were full releases: Content Warning, PEAK, Gas Station Simulator, Chained Together and WEBFISHING. Stated EA plans ranged from 6 months (Lethal Company, Liar's Bar) to about 3 years (Bellwright), and real EA ran 14–24 months for the sims. Only Schedule I (Trello) and Bellwright (roadmap promised) referenced a roadmap, and none had a dated public one. Lethal Company shipped with "7 planets, 9 creatures, 8 tools" and R.E.P.O. with "4 levels, 29 enemies".
10. **Automation and staff are the late-game reward in the sims.** TCG unlocks "tools that automate management". Gas Station lets you "leave those details to automation" and hire staff who level up. Supermarket and Schedule I add staff hires. The co-op horror hits instead use shop upgrades between quotas (R.E.P.O. Service Station, Lethal Company credits) and a cosmetics-only meta (PEAK badges, Content Warning hats).
