# Port Boss: Steam launch patterns research

Compiled 2026-10-31. Every claim was checked on the live web (search plus fetch). "Unverified" means I couldn't confirm it from a primary or reliable source. Prices are USD unless stated. Steam store pages loaded in INR from this location, so USD prices come from third-party sources.

---

## A. Early Access

**Valve's own rules and guidance**
- Early Access needs a playable game; Valve says it "is not a way to crowdfund development." Valve also says not to "make specific promises about future events." https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/earlyaccess (accessed 2026-10-31)
- The store page must say what is in the build now, what will differ at 1.0, and how pricing will change. You set a planned 1.0 date or rough timeframe. Steam shows a notice on the store page after 12 months with no update. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/earlyaccess (accessed 2026-10-31)
- No permanent discounts are allowed in EA. After a price increase you can't discount for 30 days, and a launch discount won't apply if the increase falls within 30 days of 1.0. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/earlyaccess (accessed 2026-10-31)
- A game gets Popular Upcoming only once, and an EA launch uses up that appearance. Zukowski says to get at least 7,000 wishlists even before an EA launch. https://howtomarketagame.com/?p=6327 (archived post, marked out of date June 2026) (accessed 2026-10-31)

**Data: Zukowski (How To Market A Game) and GameDiscoverCo**
- Zukowski (2023, VG Insights data from 2019 on): median revenue was $2,847 for games that reached 1.0, $847 for EA-only and $613 for normal launches. For games with 100+ reviews: $77,000 at 1.0, $62,828 EA-only, $43,500 normal. He says to treat the EA launch as the real launch, and not to do EA for money or just for feedback. https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/07/27/should-you-do-early-access (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Zukowski on EA length (games with 10+ reviews that entered and exited EA): mean about 399 days, median 260 days. Length had essentially no correlation with % positive reviews (0.0162). He says "indies should always be increasing their price"; the median price rise over EA was $4.00. https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/09/19/early-access-tips-part-3/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- GameDiscoverCo (Nov 2023): the median game sells about 0.7x as many units in its first three months of 1.0 as in its first three months of EA, with the middle 50% between 0.35x and 1.35x. "The longer you are in Early Access, the lower the 1.0 unit multiplier." https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/will-your-steam-10-launch-outpace (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Newzoo (2025 report): post-1.0 performance peaks for games that spent about 6 months in EA and drops sharply after that. https://insider-gaming.com/keeping-game-early-access-nine-months-kill-it-data/ (accessed 2026-10-31)

**How long hits stayed in EA, and how often they update**
- Lethal Company: EA from 23 Oct 2023. The developer said "I plan to finish it within 6 months" and no price change is planned. https://store.steampowered.com/app/1966720/Lethal_Company/ (accessed 2026-10-31). By Aug 2024 (v62) he wrote "I feel like I'm burning out on Lethal Company." https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/version-62-lethal-company-dev-205154573.html (accessed 2026-10-31)
- R.E.P.O.: EA from 26 Feb 2025. The EA plan was "around a year and a half." Current content is 4 levels and 29 enemies. 1.0 is planned to add meta progression, "more story elements" and more levels, and the price "might" rise at 1.0. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Schedule I: EA from 24 Mar 2025, about 2 years planned, exit listed as Q4 2027. The developer expects "substantial content updates on a monthly basis," the price "may increase slightly," and community polls decide what to add next. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3164500/Schedule_I/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Supermarket Simulator: EA 20 Feb 2024, 1.0 on 19 Jun 2025 (about 16 months). https://gameworldobserver.com/?p=23296 ; https://gamespress.westeu-v2.propressroom.com/Supermarket-Simulator-Exits-Early-Access-and-Launches-V10-on-PC (accessed 2026-10-31)
- PowerWash Simulator: EA 19 May 2021, 1.0 on 14 Jul 2022 (about 14 months). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PowerWash_Simulator (accessed 2026-10-31)
- How many hours of content a game needs at EA launch: **unverified.** I found no cross-game dataset. The store pages above describe content in levels and features, not hours.

## B. Demo and Steam Next Fest

**Next Fest dates (Steamworks)**
- **Feb 2027:** 22 Feb to 1 Mar 2027. Registration deadline **10 Jan 2027, 11:59pm PST**. Press Preview submission 18 Jan; all items submitted for review by 1 Feb; Press Preview starts 11 Feb. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest/feb_2027 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **June 2027:** 14 to 21 Jun 2027. Registration deadline **25 Apr 2027, 11:59pm PDT**. Trailer pull 3 May; demo submitted for Press Preview by 10 May; all items by 24 May; Press Preview starts 3 Jun. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest/june_2027 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Rules:** a game can enter only one Next Fest ("titles may only participate in ONE Next Fest"). Unreleased Early Access titles qualify. Releasing before or during the fest makes the game ineligible. Valve suggests keeping the demo up after the fest. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest (accessed 2026-10-31)

**How many wishlists Next Fest adds**
- Feb 2026 survey (182 responses), median wishlists gained during the fest, by wishlists held the day before: 322 (0 to 999 before), 1,006 (1k to 9.9k), 5,215 (10k to 99k), 12,882 (100k+). Pre-fest wishlists correlate with fest gains at r=0.825. Zukowski's minimum: "2000+ wishlists before SNF." https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/03/26/benchmarks-how-many-wishlists-can-i-get-from-steam-next-fest/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Overall Feb 2026 median was 806 (1,079 in Feb 2025); 70th percentile 1,839, 95th 13,461. Co-op games have "the lowest ratio of plays to wishlists," and friend-slop titles dominated the Feb 2026 charts. Advice: "You should do the last Next Fest before you launch." https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/04/13/making-sense-of-the-february-2026-steam-next-fest/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- GameDiscoverCo's broader sample puts the median near 200 wishlists. **Unverified at the primary source**; it's cited secondhand at https://presskit.gg/blog/steam-next-fest-2026-results (accessed 2026-10-31)
- June 2026 had 4,382 demos (2,645 in June 2025). The top 10% of games gained +121 followers (about 3k wishlists), down about 25% year on year. Casual co-op has "room for more" standouts. https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-june-2026s-steam-next-fest (accessed 2026-10-31)
- October 2026 had 7,240 games, about a third with AI disclosures. Outside the top 100, the median game had 15 followers the day before the fest. https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/10/21/how-sloppy-is-the-october-2026-steam-next-fest/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Zukowski also finds that festival wishlists convert worse than other wishlists. https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/10/13/wishlist-inflation-is-real-but-they-are-still-important/ (accessed 2026-10-31)

**When to release a demo, and how long it should be**
- Games that released a demo well before the fest earned 2.5x more wishlists, though the correlation is weak (r=-0.205). Of the 10 games that gained 15k+ wishlists, 5 had demos out months ahead, 4 about a month ahead and 1 shadow-dropped. https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/04/13/making-sense-of-the-february-2026-steam-next-fest/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- With AI clones spreading, Zukowski says: make a demo, get it to creators first, and don't shadow-drop. https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/10/28/ai-cloners-are-here-are-indie-devs-cooked/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- GameDiscoverCo says the "short, sharp" demo is the default. A long-available demo only works with "a standout (but short!) gameplay loop" (Loop Hero, Riftbreaker). https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/when-demos-can-radically-expand-your (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Valve gives no recommended demo length. The often-quoted 20 to 40 minute target comes only from secondary guides. **Unverified.** https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/how-long-should-a-steam-demo-be (accessed 2026-10-31)

**What top co-op and job-sim demos did**
- Crashout Crew (Aggro Crab, 4-player co-op forklift game): Next Fest demo 13 to 20 Oct 2025 with 3 levels, then pulled after the fest. Released 28 May 2026. Wishlist numbers: **unverified.** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crashout_Crew ; https://www.gematsu.com/2025/10/co-op-forklift-game-crashout-crew-announced-for-pc (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Supermarket Simulator: demo and prologue in Next Fest, one of the 50 most-played demos, over 33k wishlists just before EA launch, $12.99 with 20% off at launch. It "didn't instantly go viral": CCU went from about 3k on day 1 to 38k two weeks later, driven by Twitch and YouTube. https://gameworldobserver.com/?p=23296 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Roadside Research (co-op gas-station job sim, $13): 250k wishlists by Sep 2025, 903k unique demo players, median demo session 1h12m, 396k units sold. https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-two-friendslop-hits-got-the-audience (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Parcel Simulator (job sim): demo released 20 Feb 2025 just before Next Fest; got "0-3 wishlists per day" before the demo; sold 55k+ units with about $50 of marketing spend (Keymailer). https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-parcel-simulator-stealthily-racked (accessed 2026-10-31)
- PEAK, R.E.P.O. and Schedule I: I found no evidence of a Next Fest demo. **Unverified.** Schedule I has a separate "Free Sample" demo listing. https://en.number13.de/schedule-1-how-much-costs-the-new-game-on-steam/ (accessed 2026-10-31)

## C. Wishlist targets and conversion

- **7,000 wishlists** is Zukowski's old launch minimum ("Minimum is 7000, or thereabouts"); Popular Upcoming covers games releasing within the next 7 days. The post is now marked archived and out of date (June 2026). https://howtomarketagame.com/?p=6327 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Zukowski (13 Oct 2026): 2023 to 2025 releases sold "about half" as many units per wishlist in their first month as 2020 to 2022 releases. Wishlists still predict sales just as well. https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/10/13/wishlist-inflation-is-real-but-they-are-still-important/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Coverage of that analysis gives first-month sales per wishlist of **0.45** (2020 to 2022) vs **0.22** (2023 to 2025), and 0.82 vs 0.43 at 6 months. https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27273/steam-wishlist-inflation-value-of-1-wishlist-cut-in-half-but-predictive-power-remains-unchanged (accessed 2026-10-31)
- GameDiscoverCo (Oct 2025), first-week sales per wishlist for games launching with over 25k wishlists: median **0.15x**; **0.10x for games priced over $10**. Many of the top converters were "online co-op centric." https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-state-of-steam-wishlist-conversions (accessed 2026-10-31)
- What that means: 7,000 wishlists × 0.10 to 0.15 ≈ **700 to 1,050 first-week units**. 25,000 × 0.15 ≈ 3,750. (My arithmetic on the cited ratios.)
- Song of Iron case study: 81k net wishlists at launch, average cohort conversion about 8%, Next Fest cohort 5.51%. https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/01/27/do-wishlists-get-old/ (accessed 2026-10-31)

## D. Pricing

- Launch prices of friendslop hits: Lethal Company $9.99, R.E.P.O. $9.99, PEAK $7.99, RV There Yet? $7.99, Content Warning $7.99, Guilty as Sock! $5.50. https://appmagic.rocks/blog/friendslop-steam-games-2025 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- 2026 breakouts: Meccha Chameleon at $6 (10M+ copies, $50M+ gross) https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/look-we-have-to-talk-about-meccha ; Super Battle Golf at $8 (500k units in 9 days) https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-two-friendslop-hits-got-the-audience (accessed 2026-10-31)
- PEAK developer Nick Kaman: "eight bucks is still five bucks. It doesn't become ten bucks." PEAK launched at a reduced price off an $8 list price, copying Content Warning's price. https://www.gamefile.news/p/peak-interview (7 Jan 2026) (accessed 2026-10-31). The console version is $9.99 vs $7.99 on PC; the team justified it by content having doubled. https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-52483 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Price and conversion: GameDiscoverCo's median wishlist conversion falls to 0.10x above $10, from 0.15x overall. That is a correlation, not proof that cheap prices cause virality. https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-state-of-steam-wishlist-conversions (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Group-buy effect: friends buying together means "the effective spend at least doubles." The low price "makes players more forgiving" in reviews. https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-friendslop-games-in-2025 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Industry critique (Dumbbell Games, Jun 2026): friendslop has trained buyers to expect sub-$10 games and makes higher indie prices hard to justify. https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-50997 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Steam discount rules:** launch discount up to **40%**, running 7 to 14 days; regular discounts 10% to 95%; 30-day cooldown between discounts (seasonal sales exempt). https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/discounts (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Common launch-discount practice of 10% to 20%: found only in secondary guides; I couldn't find it in Valve's docs. **Unverified.** https://bugnet.io/blog/steam-launch-discounts-how-deep-should-you-go (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Regional pricing:** Steam has 37 currencies and a Propose Pricing tool (exchange-rate, purchasing-power or multi-variable conversion). At the $4.99 tier the maximum discount is 90%; price changes should wait 30 days after release. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/pricing (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Brazil was over 30% of one Next Fest winner's demo players (Alinea, cited secondhand). https://gameindustrylibrary.com/research/shared/6-how-has-the-friendslop-genre-evolved (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **4-pack psychology:** I found no data on 4-pack effects; only examples of co-op bundles exist (e.g. Humble "Better With 4 Friends"). **Unverified.** https://steamdeckhq.com/news/better-with-4-friends-humble-bundle/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- $4.99 vs $6.99 vs $9.99 head to head: no controlled data found. **Unverified.**

## E. The friendslop genre

**What makes these games break out**
- AppMagic (Mar 2026) lists: proximity chat ("co-op + proximity chat + unpredictable reactions"), clumsy physics ("more about falling than climbing"), $5 to $20 prices with chain group-buying, building games to "own a handful of evenings," moments that look good in a TikTok clip or stream, and "almost no onboarding." https://appmagic.rocks/blog/friendslop-steam-games-2025 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Zukowski (Jul 2026): a "funny fantasy" matters more than polish; players tolerate "jank"; the audience is "omnivorous" and "hungry," not saturated. Top titles "take over 50% of the playerbase then fade to 10% within 3-9 months." https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/07/30/is-friendslop-saturated (accessed 2026-10-31)
- GameDiscoverCo on Meccha Chameleon: an easy-to-grasp concept at $6, built on an existing multiplayer framework with no dedicated servers, and streamer-friendly "playacting." https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/look-we-have-to-talk-about-meccha (accessed 2026-10-31)
- 4 of the top 10 Steam premium sellers of 2025 by copies were friendslop: R.E.P.O. 18.5M, PEAK 15.4M, Schedule I 9.1M, RV There Yet? 5.7M (AppMagic). https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-friendslop-games-in-2025 (accessed 2026-10-31)

**Critiques and evidence of saturation**
- GameDiscoverCo (Jun 2026): "There are very few middling friendslop hits." The odds compare to "being struck by lightning." https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/look-we-have-to-talk-about-meccha (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Retention: average D30 is about 3% (Dead by Daylight 11.3%, Phasmophobia 5.3%). https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-friendslop-games-in-2025 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- The A.V. Club (Jul 2025) calls it a "rapidly-oversaturating" subgenre with many games "rushed out to cash in on the trend." https://www.avclub.com/not-every-co-op-game-is-friendslop (accessed 2026-10-31)
- The label has moved from derogatory toward descriptive (IGN May 2026, Polygon Feb 2026, as cited by Wikipedia). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friendslop (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Jonathan Blow's view: it looks popular because "there's so much of it and 5% of it succeeds." https://gameindustrylibrary.com/research/shared/6-how-has-the-friendslop-genre-evolved (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Next Fest volume keeps growing: 7,240 entries in Oct 2026, up 65% on June 2026. https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/10/21/how-sloppy-is-the-october-2026-steam-next-fest/ (accessed 2026-10-31)

## F. Review complaints that sank similar games

- **Servers, always-online and less content than the demo:** Party Animals launched "Mostly Negative," recovered to Mixed at 57% positive, despite a 104k CCU peak. Complaints were 10+ minute server queues, an always-online requirement that broke an implied offline mode, and less content (maps) than earlier demos. https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/09/21/party-animals-100k-ccu-negative-reviews-china-steam (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Physics netcode:** Gang Beasts' 1.0 review (Shacknews, 6/10) reported "trouble keeping everyone connected," "fighters warping around, and a lot of lag." https://www.shacknews.com/article/102538/gang-beasts-review-royal-fumble (accessed 2026-10-31). The developer's own notes said beta issues "compromised online and physics stability." https://gangbeasts.game/posts/2017/5/30/patch-notes-for-the-gang-beasts-051-beta-and-information-on-issues-with-the-050-beta-build (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Host-only progress:** in Schedule I, "only the host can save" and "the host owns the world" (community posts, Mar/Apr 2025). https://steamcommunity.com/app/3164500/discussions/1/599648576856983509 (accessed 2026-10-31). The same complaint recurs for Atlas Fallen and Evil West. https://steamcommunity.com/app/1230530/discussions/0/6363075944119256933 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Update drought, seen as abandonment:** Lethal Company forum threads ask whether the game is abandoned, citing an early promise of weekly updates; the developer reported burnout in Aug 2024. https://steamcommunity.com/app/1966720/discussions/0/591761374510599270 ; https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/version-62-lethal-company-dev-205154573.html (accessed 2026-10-31). An industry view (Jun 2026): "a game without updates within weeks may look abandoned." https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-50997 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Repetition and short lifespan:** players "have a great time for a couple of weeks, and then move on," because these games lack "deep progression or meta systems." https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-friendslop-games-in-2025 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Price vs hours:** one Steam reviewer of a six-mission co-op game says "there simply isn't enough content to justify that price." That is a single review, so treat it as anecdotal. https://steamcommunity.com/id/BasicBrit/reviews (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **No rejoin after disconnect:** this comes only from AI-generated review summaries (Lost Castle 2). **Low confidence.** https://vaporlens.app/app/2445690/lost_castle_2 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- The claim that Content Warning has mostly negative reviews (Vaporlens) contradicts the game's known reception. **Unverified; excluded.**

## G. Story: do co-op job, sim and friendslop games need one?

**Light framings that worked**
- **A quota boss.** Lethal Company: contract workers for "the Company" must meet rising profit quotas; missing one ejects the crew into space. It has a fail-state rather than an ending, and lore hints come from bestiary and journal entries. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethal_Company ; https://store.steampowered.com/app/1966720/Lethal_Company/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **An emoji overseer.** R.E.P.O.: the Steam page says you work for "a mysterious computer intelligence," retrieving valuables. Guides call it the "Taxman," who speaks in emojis on a screen in the truck. The name is **verified only through secondary guides** (Dot Esports and Gamerblurb, via search snippets; their pages returned 403 when fetched). A **debt framing is unverified**. The lore is deliberately vague. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/ ; https://shapes.inc/guides/r-e-p-o-characters-explained (accessed 2026-10-31). The 1.0 roadmap adds "more story elements," so the developers see story as something to add later, not a launch requirement. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **An inherited farm and a corporate rival.** Stardew Valley: you inherit your late grandfather's farm; the Joja Corporation is the antagonist; you restore the Community Center; 50M+ sold (Feb 2026). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stardew_Valley (accessed 2026-10-31). Ranch Simulator (4-player co-op) uses the same inherited-ranch hook. https://www.co-op.gg/pc/game/ranch-simulator (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **A hidden mystery in text messages.** PowerWash Simulator: client texts during jobs slowly reveal the mayor diverting the town's water, missing cats and a volcano plot. Supports online co-op. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PowerWash_Simulator (accessed 2026-10-31). The developers describe the story as "almost optional" and something that "can happily be ignored" (quoted secondhand from a TouchArcade interview; the original returned 403). https://toucharcade.com/2023/03/02/powerwash-simulator-interview-lead-designer-final-fantasy-7-dlc-midgar-next-game-ios-port-switch-gyro-support-ps5-dualsense/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **A mystery delivered through notes.** Dredge: messages in bottles carry the mystery; critics praised its "mysteriousness and open-endedness"; 100k sold in 24 hours and 1M by Oct 2023. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dredge_(video_game) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **No story at all.** Schedule I's framing is a rise from small-time dealer to kingpin against rivals, with no cutscenes listed. Supermarket Simulator and PEAK have no narrative framing listed on their store pages or in reviews. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3164500/Schedule_I/ (accessed 2026-10-31)

**Talks and data on forced story in co-op**
- The GDC Online 2011 panel "Game Together or Die Alone" said narrative with drop-in/drop-out co-op "can be uniquely challenging." https://gdcvault.com/play/1015134/Game-Together-or-Die-Alone (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Players in co-op games repeatedly ask for vote-to-skip or pausing time during cutscenes (Stardew, FFXIV forums). This is anecdotal, not data. https://forums.stardewvalley.net/goto/post?id=9386 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- Quantitative data on cutscene skip rates in co-op: none found. **Unverified.**

## Recommendations for Port Boss

1. **Register for Next Fest June 2027 (deadline 25 Apr 2027), not Feb 2027 (deadline 10 Jan 2027, 10 weeks away).** You only get one Next Fest, it should be the last one before launch, and you want 2,000+ wishlists going in. (B: Steamworks; Zukowski SNF benchmarks)
2. **Put the public demo out months before the fest**, by about Feb or Mar 2027, and send it to creators first. Early demos earned 2.5x more wishlists, and Zukowski warns against shadow drops. (B: Zukowski Feb 2026 SNF; AI cloners)
3. **Make the demo one short, replayable loop:** a single pier with one ship departing on the clock. GameDiscoverCo says "short, sharp" unless the loop is a standout. Roadside Research shows a strong job-sim demo can hold players for a median 1h12m. (B: GameDiscoverCo)
4. **Target at least 7,000 wishlists before the EA launch, and plan around 0.10 to 0.15x first-week conversion** (about 700 to 1,050 units at 7,000 wishlists). Festival wishlists convert worse, so don't count Next Fest gains at full value. (C: Zukowski; GameDiscoverCo)
5. **Treat the EA launch as the real launch.** It uses your single Popular Upcoming appearance. (A: Zukowski)
6. **Keep $6.99.** It sits in the "$8 is still $5" band with Meccha Chameleon ($6) and PEAK ($7.99), and below the $10 line where conversion drops to 0.10x. Use Valve's regional pricing tool rather than raw exchange rates. (D: Game File; GameDiscoverCo; Steamworks)
7. **Launch discount: use a modest one, well under Valve's 40% cap.** Plan to raise the price at 1.0 and announce that plan in the EA Q&A. Remember the 30-day rules around price increases and discounts. (A, D: Steamworks; Zukowski "always increasing")
8. **Plan EA at about 6 to 12 months**, with a public monthly cadence like Schedule I's. Long EA lowers the 1.0 multiplier, and "no update in weeks" reads as abandonment. (A: Newzoo; GameDiscoverCo; F: Lethal Company forums)
9. **Ship drop-in rejoin and shared or guest-persistent port progress from day one.** Host-only saves are a recurring complaint, and the growing port is Port Boss's retention answer to friendslop's roughly 3% D30. (F: Schedule I forum; AppMagic)
10. **Test physics netcode under real latency before the demo.** Desynced ragdolls and warping killed Gang Beasts' online reviews, and Party Animals' server queues and always-online requirement sank its launch reviews. (F: Shacknews; Game World Observer)
11. **Use a light frame, not a forced story.** One quota boss (the Harbourmaster or the Company) plus an optional mystery delivered through manifests or radio messages, PowerWash-style, which players can ignore. No unskippable cutscenes in co-op. (G: Lethal Company; PowerWash; GDC 2011)
12. **Build for clips:** proximity voice, ships leaving with half-stacked cargo, crates thrown into the water. Streamers and short clips drove every breakout cited, including the slow-burn ones. (E: AppMagic; B: Supermarket Simulator)
