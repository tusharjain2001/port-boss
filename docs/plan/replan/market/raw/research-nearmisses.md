# Near-misses and port/logistics niche: research notes

Accessed 2026-10-31. Every bullet ends with its source. "unverified" means I found no live source for it. Review quotes come from Steam's English review pages, sorted by top-rated, all reviews or negative only. This file leaves out review counts, prices, release dates, CCU and sales on purpose; those live in other files.

Review URL pattern: `https://steamcommunity.com/app/<id>/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english` (negative) and `.../reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english` (all).

## 1. Crashout Crew (Aggro Crab, 3583210): the closest comp, covered in most depth

- **Team:** Aggro Crab, self-published. I found no headcount, so it is unverified. The Head of Publishing said the studio was "not really interested in significantly growing the size of our team". https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/aggro-crab-launches-publishing-label-to-splash-that-peak-cash (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Story:** light framing, a workplace satire. You work at "DE NILE SHIPPING", a warehouse that puts speed ahead of safety, and damage comes out of your pay. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3583210/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Objectives:** load a set number of items into trucks before the trucks leave. This is the same departure-clock mechanic as Port Boss's ships. Players who hit hazards build stress, and a stressed player "crashes out" and drives erratically. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crashout_Crew (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Structure and length:** "about 8–10 hours", "20+ contracts with 5 shifts each", S-rank challenges, and optional "Safety Violations" modifiers. https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=3583210&count=15&maxlength=1500 (release-date post, 2026-04-23) (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Co-op:** 1-4 players, online lobbies only. There is no local co-op, which has its own "no local co-op" forum thread. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3583210/ ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/3583210/discussions/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Progression:** only within a run. Warehouse devices and forklift upgrades are bought with earnings, and they "reset after the contract". Contracts unlock by rating. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3583210/ ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/3583210/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Marketing:** announced 3 Oct 2025, with a Steam Next Fest demo (Oct 2025) holding the first 3 contracts. The demo was extended to 27 Oct and then pulled so the team could rework the game from feedback. Launch leaned on "from the makers of PEAK" press. https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=3583210&count=15&maxlength=1500 ; https://www.gematsu.com/2025/10/co-op-forklift-game-crashout-crew-announced-for-pc (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Game Pass:** the game launched straight into Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass, alongside Xbox Series. https://www.thexboxhub.com/crashout-crew-lands-on-game-pass/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Top negative 1, content runs out and nothing new unlocks:**
  - "gets extremely repetitive after the first 10 levels nothing new gets unlocked except cosmetics" (11.6 h)
  - "if you've played 3 hours of this game, you have effectively seen everything it has to offer" (14.8 h)
  - "lacking in content for a game that the devs say they have no intention of adding to" (2.3 h)

  https://steamcommunity.com/app/3583210/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Top negative 2, co-op does not scale or reward teamwork:**
  - "cant beat the later levels with only two people" (11.4 h)
  - "almost no reason to work together with 2 players?" (2.8 h)
  - "the lack of local Coop a big downside" (5.6 h)

  Same source (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Top negative 3, hazards become tedious rather than fun:**
  - "just becomes really annoyingly tedious the more you play" (5.9 h)
  - Some shop items are far more useful than others.

  Same source (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Why players stopped:**
  - Most negative reviewers quit after 2-15 h, once they had seen all the content.
  - Crashes are the top issue in an AI review aggregator (secondary source; treat as indicative only). https://vaporlens.app/app/3583210/crashout_crew (accessed 2026-10-31)
  - Players could not rejoin after a disconnect. https://steamcommunity.com/app/3583210/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **What players asked for:**
  - a randomised endless mode
  - rejoining a match after a disconnect
  - local co-op
  - more to buy with money ("money has little to buy")

  https://steamcommunity.com/app/3583210/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/3583210/discussions/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Dev response after launch:**
  - Patches 1.2.4, 1.3.6 and 1.3.7 handled balance, accessibility symbols for colourblind players and a backup save system.
  - 1.3.7 admitted the final contract was too hard, raised its timer and removed the penalty for damaged boxes.
  - The same post announced Youtooz merch.
  - None of these posts adds content or a roadmap.

  https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=3583210&count=15&maxlength=1500 (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Post-mortem or sales interview:** none found, so unverified. The reviewer's claim that the devs have "no intention of adding" content has no dev statement I could find, so it is also unverified.

## 2. Docked (Saber Interactive, 2487300): covered in most depth

- **Team:** Saber Interactive developed and published it, and the team behind it is unnamed. Headcount is unverified. https://store.steampowered.com/app/2487300/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Story:** a narrative campaign. You "rebuild Port Wake after a devastating hurricane" and save the family business. Several reviewers found the story intrusive. https://store.steampowered.com/app/2487300/ ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/2487300/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Objectives:** realistic machinery: ship-to-shore and RTG cranes, terminal tractors, reach stackers and straddle carriers. Contracts are worked off a cargo-plan tablet and milestones gate progress. https://store.steampowered.com/app/2487300/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Length:** reviewers estimate the campaign at about 10 h ("a 30-40€ 10 hour tutorial"), about 18 h, and "12 hours" at most. https://steamcommunity.com/app/2487300/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Co-op:** none. Steam lists Single-player only, and co-op and multiplayer are top player requests. https://store.steampowered.com/app/2487300/ ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/2487300/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Progression:** milestone-based on a "predetermined" port layout. There is a per-day mission cap and milestone timers, and taking missions in the wrong order can soft-lock a save. https://steamcommunity.com/app/2487300/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Marketing:**
  - The Steam Next Fest demo (Oct 2025) was extended after a "strong response".
  - Further demo updates followed, and trailers ran from Jan to Feb 2026.
  - Pre-orders came with a Premium Reach Stacker, and a Deluxe Edition bundles the "New Horizons" expansion.
  - It launched on PC, PS5 and Xbox, and a physical Deluxe Edition followed on 22 Oct.

  https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=2487300&count=20&maxlength=1200 ; https://kickpush.prezly.com/docked-deluxe-edition-arrives-on-october-22-for-playstationr5-xbox-series (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Top negative 1, thin content and jobs that move only a few containers:**
  - "it's a tutorial. It's a 30-40€ 10 hour tutorial."
  - "missions… are generally WAY to short"
  - "a very pretty spreadsheet simulator"

  https://steamcommunity.com/app/2487300/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Top negative 2, minigames as filler:** "This is just a bunch of minigames put together." Same source (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Top negative 3, scripted progression with no real management and DLC pushed early:**
  - "progression is completely milestone-based and the port layout is already predetermined"
  - "I've bought the entrance ticket to a DLC store"

  Same source (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Why players stopped or refunded:**
  - "I ended up requesting a refund after 3hrs" (3.2 h). A second reviewer requested a refund at 16.2 h.
  - Others quit at the end of the campaign because there was no replay or free play, or because of soft-locks from mission order.

  https://steamcommunity.com/app/2487300/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/2487300/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **What players asked for:**
  - free play and replayable missions
  - drop-in co-op hosted by a player
  - a "dynamic", living port
  - full-ship bulk unloads
  - optional minigames
  - no milestone timers

  https://steamcommunity.com/app/2487300/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Dev response, a recovery arc:**
  - 1.1.1 (7 Apr) added Open Port and an All Jobs replay menu.
  - 1.2.0 (21 Apr) made minigames optional.
  - 1.3.0 (11 May) added Free Play v1 and joystick support.
  - Two paid DLCs followed: Deep Waters (10 Jun) and Contraband (28 Jul).
  - One top positive review: "first time i see dev actually listen to the players".

  https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=2487300&count=20&maxlength=1200 ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/2487300/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Post-mortem or sales statement:** none found, so unverified.

## 4. Container Terminal Simulator (Tikam Studio, 3806270)

- **Basics:**
  - Single-player only, so it is not co-op.
  - It came out of Early Access into 1.0.
  - There is no story; you grow "a small terminal yard" into a "modern logistics hub".
  - The loop is receiving container trains, unloading them and managing the yard. Money is reinvested in equipment, and double-stack trains unlock later.

  https://store.steampowered.com/app/3806270/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Team and marketing:** team size is unverified, and no demo or Next Fest is mentioned. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3806270/ (accessed 2026-10-31)
- **Top negatives:** only 3 negative reviews were visible, so this is a thin sample.
  1. Containers must land almost perfectly to snap into place.
  2. Bugs persist after 1.0 ("my handheld scanner has stopped showing… container number").
  3. 1.0 "changed nothing but a new UI".

  https://steamcommunity.com/app/3806270/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)

## 5. Mars First Logistics (Shape Shop / Outersloth, 1532200)

- **Team:** a "small team" led by Ian MacLarty in Melbourne, funded by Outersloth, the fund from the Among Us team. The exact headcount is unverified. https://www.pushtotalk.gg/p/after-releasing-50-games-this-australian (accessed 2026-10-31)

  1. Floaty, bouncy physics and hard-to-read terrain ("you fight the game more than play it").
  2. Repetitive deliveries ("The charm wears off in less than two hours").
  3. A clunky build interface ("so damn clunky").

  https://steamcommunity.com/app/1532200/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english (accessed 2026-10-31)

## What killed / limited these games

## Port niche map: who is where

Sources are the sections above.

| | Single-player | Online co-op |
|---|---|---|
| **Realistic sim** | Docked, Container Terminal Sim, Port Logistics Sim, Port Authority, Harbor Master | Seafarer co-op update, Loading Dock Simulator (upcoming) |
| **Stylised / comedic physics, port or sea setting** | Ridiculous Shipping, Tiny Harbor, Fishing Co. Tycoon | Holey Ship (repair shop, tiny footprint), Cargo Thieves (port heist, upcoming), Harpoon Crew (fish shop, upcoming), Water You Doing? |
| **Stylised co-op logistics, not a port** | Shipping Store Sim 2037 | Crashout Crew (warehouse; trucks leave on a clock), Moving Out 2, TRDS, Mars First Logistics |

The gap: I found no released game with stylised 1-4 co-op physics stevedoring, ships leaving on a clock, and a port that persists and grows. Crashout Crew is the nearest mechanic (vehicles leave on a clock), but it resets progress per contract. Docked is the nearest setting, but it is realistic and single-player. Cargo Thieves is the nearest upcoming co-op port game, with a theft framing.
