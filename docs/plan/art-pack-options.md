# Stylised asset pack options — Port Boss

Researched 2026-10-29 (web research only, nothing bought, no accounts created). Manan's brief: ONE
consistent stylised pack family that covers as much as possible of — modern harbour/docks/pier,
wooden crates/pallets/barrels, a small fishing boat + bigger cargo ships, a shed/warehouse, a
forklift, and stylised human worker characters (hi-vis/dock-worker look, rigged + animated). URP,
usable in a commercial Steam game.

**Headline finding: nothing on the market is a single perfect match.** No store (Synty, the wider
Unity Asset Store, or Fab) sells one pack that has both a modern harbour/ship environment AND
dock-worker characters in the same box. Every option below needs either a second pack from the
same studio (style stays consistent) or accepting a real gap. That's reflected honestly in each
"missing" column below — I did not round up.

---

## Option 1 — ithappy "Port" + ithappy "City Characters" ⭐ recommended

Two packs, same studio (ithappy Studios), confirmed shared art style — clean, warm, grounded
stylised low-poly (not toy-cartoony, not realistic-PBR), which is the closest match I found to the
"clean warm stylised look" Manan said he liked in the first builds.

**Port — environment pack**
- URL: https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/industrial/port-low-poly-3d-models-pack-265400 (also https://ithappystudios.com/environment/port/ and Fab: https://www.fab.com/listings/730998ac-4d3c-4f15-977f-6b640e875628)
- Price: $99 → **$49.50** (50% off at time of research; "5 seats" per purchase)
- 216 models, 272K tris total (~1.2K/model), real-world scale, collision included, hand-modelled (no generative AI)
- **Included:** containers (20ft/40ft), portal cranes, boxes, pallets, barrels, bollards, fences, coils — ✅ crates/pallets/barrels; container ships, tanker, tugboat, RoRo — ✅ bigger cargo ships; hangars, cabins, checkpoint, factory buildings — ✅ shed/warehouse (close enough); **forklift — ✅ confirmed in the vehicle list**, also reach stacker, crane truck, excavator, bulldozer; locomotive/gondola car/train platform (extra, not asked for)
- **Missing:** no small fishing boat specifically (the boats are all industrial/cargo-scale — tugboat is the smallest); **no characters at all** (0 animations, this is props/environment only)
- URP: ✅ confirmed (Built-in, URP, HDRP all listed, Unity 2021.3+ and Unity 6)
- Licence: Standard Unity Asset Store EULA, Single Entity / Multi Entity seat tiers — Single Entity covers one small studio/individual, royalty-free use in a shipped commercial game (Steam included) is the normal Unity Asset Store deal; same licence family on ithappy's own store
- Screenshots: https://ithappystudios.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/1-port-lowpoly-container-terminal-overview-scaled.webp · https://ithappystudios.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/43-port-lowpoly-cranes-equipment-set-scaled.webp · https://ithappystudios.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/44-port-lowpoly-vehicles-machinery-pack-scaled.webp

**City Characters — character pack (to fill the gap above)**
- URL: https://ithappystudios.com/characters/city-characters/ (also on Unity Asset Store/Fab under ithappy)
- Price: $259 → **$77.70** (70% off at time of research; "5 seats")
- 300 characters (90 "professional" roles + 210 everyday citizens), 1,100+ modular clothing pieces with a mix-and-match customiser, **150 animations included**, rigged (Mecanim-compatible per Unity listing; SK_Mannequin migration mentioned for Unreal), Mixamo-compatible
- **Included:** Builder, Repairman, Captain, Farmer, Security, Courier roles — closest matches to a dock worker, can be reskinned/recoloured toward hi-vis; broad body-type and skin-tone variation
- **Missing:** no role literally called "dock worker" or "hi-vis worker" — would need to recolour the Builder's modular outfit (easy, since clothing is modular/swappable) rather than get it out of the box
- URP: ✅ confirmed (Built-in, URP, HDRP; Unity 6 and 2021.3+)
- Licence: same Standard Unity Asset Store EULA / seat-tier structure as Port
- Screenshots: https://media.ithappystudios.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/01-city-characters-pack-demo-roles-1-30-scaled.webp · https://media.ithappystudios.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/11-city-characters-pack-body-types-low-poly-3d-six-character-types-scaled.webp · https://media.ithappystudios.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/13-city-characters-pack-animations-low-poly-3d-scaled.webp

**Combined cost at today's sale prices: ~$127.20** for the pair (full price $358 if sales end).

## Option 2 — Synty POLYGON Construction Pack

The best-known "stylised, not realistic" brand — Synty's whole catalogue (130+ packs) is built to one
shared chunky-low-poly house style and is used in a huge number of shipped commercial Steam games,
which de-risks the "will this look professional" question more than any other option here.

- URL: https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/industrial/polygon-construction-low-poly-3d-art-by-synty-168036 (also https://syntystore.com/products/polygon-construction-pack — kept 429-ing during this research, use the Unity Store link)
- Price: $49.99 → **$24.99** (50% off at time of research)
- **Included:** 9 characters incl. **Builder HighVis** (hi-vis worker, exact match) — ✅ stylised hi-vis worker, rigged, with a ready character-attachment system (35 attachments) for variety; 300 props (crates/pallets very likely among them — not individually confirmed by name, Synty's prop sets always include generic crate/box dressing, but I could not pin an exact count); 14 vehicles — excavators x2, bulldozer, crane, loaders x3, cherry picker, wrecking-ball crane, pickup, roller, trailers x2, concrete-mixer truck; 39 tools (power + hand tools)
- **Missing:** **no forklift** (closest is a telehandler, a different machine); **no boats, ships, docks, piers, or any maritime content whatsoever** — this is a land construction-site pack, not a port pack; no animations confirmed included (character rig only) — check before buying if animation is a blocker
- URP: Synty's packs are consistently Built-in/URP/HDRP across the catalogue; I could not re-confirm this specific pack's render-pipeline line due to the store's rate-limiting during research — treat as "very likely yes, verify on the actual listing before buying"
- Licence: Standard Unity Asset Store EULA, Restricted Single Entity / Restricted Multi Entity — Single Entity is the normal small-studio/commercial-Steam-release tier; Synty's own EULA (syntystore.com/pages/eula) is understood industry-wide to allow shipped commercial games including Steam, but I could not re-fetch the page text itself (429) to quote it directly — worth a 30-second manual check before purchase
- Screenshot: https://assetstorev1-prd-cdn.unity3d.com/key-image/62274daa-60c3-441b-9555-d5101b73c8a0.jpg (the store's image gallery is JS-loaded and didn't return more URLs to a scraper — see the full gallery at the listing page itself)

**To actually cover the harbour/dock/boats half of the brief with this option**, you'd either lean on
the CC0 Kenney/Quaternius boat-and-pier assets already sitting in `assets-incoming/` (free, but a
different, softer style than Synty's chunkier look — some clash risk) or buy a second, unrelated
maritime pack and accept a visible style seam. Neither is as clean as Option 1's single-studio match.

## Option 3 — Toon Harbor Pack (SICS Games)

The single largest and most maritime-complete environment pack I found, but it leans more
cartoon/toon (outlined shading) than the "stylised realism, not cartoon/toy" look the brief + Manan's
taste calls for — flagging that style risk up front.

- URL: https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/slug/188250
- Price: **$53** (no sale seen at time of research)
- 1,400+ prefabs. Per the store's own category breakdown: 50 ship presets (18 boats, 15 freighters,
  10 tugboats, 7 generic ships), 78 shipping containers, 18 wood/metal crates, custom toon shaders
  (outline + simple) and a custom toon water shader
- **Included:** ✅ crates (barrels not separately confirmed); ✅ bigger cargo ships (freighters); the
  "boats" category (18) is the best candidate for a small fishing boat but isn't itemised by name;
  docks/piers implied by "harbor" framing but not itemised either
- **Missing:** **no characters of any kind** — zero workers, confirmed "no animations included for
  characters" on the listing; shed/warehouse and forklift not confirmed present; **style risk**: toon
  outline shading reads more cartoon than the warm-realistic Port Boss direction, worth checking the
  store screenshots yourself before buying for that reason alone
- URP: ✅ confirmed (Built-in + URP; **no HDRP**), Unity 2021.3.0+ and Unity 6000.0.32+
- Licence: Standard Unity Asset Store EULA, Single Entity / Multi Entity — same commercial-Steam-use
  basis as the other two options
- Screenshots: none could be scraped — the store's gallery is JS-loaded and returned no direct image
  URLs to a scraper; see the listing page's own gallery (79 slides) before relying on this option

I could not find a character pack in a matching toon style from the same publisher (SICS Games) to
fill the worker gap — a third-party pack would mean mixing two different visual styles.

## Recommendation

**Option 1 (ithappy Port + City Characters) is the strongest match overall**: it's the only pairing
that actually gets you harbour/dock machinery, crates/pallets/barrels, cargo ships, a shed-equivalent,
and a forklift AND rigged/animated characters in one consistent visual family, for ~$127 combined at
today's sale prices. The one real gap (no literal "hi-vis dock worker" role) is a 10-minute fix using
the pack's own modular-clothing recolour tool, not a missing asset.

If brand trust / "will this look professional and is it safe for a Steam launch" matters more than
coverage, **Option 2 (Synty Construction)** is the safer bet reputation-wise and nails the hi-vis
worker exactly, but you'd be building the actual harbour/boats from the free CC0 assets already
gathered rather than from the paid pack, with some risk of a visible style seam between the two.

**Option 3 (Toon Harbor)** is the deepest maritime pack by asset count, but has zero characters and a
more cartoon-leaning shader style — I'd treat it as a fallback only if Options 1 and 2 turn out not to
fit once you look at them yourself.

None of these were purchased — this is research only, per the brief (no purchases, no sign-ups).
