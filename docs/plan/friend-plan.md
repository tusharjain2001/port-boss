PORT BOSS ⚓ (working title)
Start with a broken wooden pier and $500. Grow it into the biggest port on the coast.

The main loop

SHIP ARRIVES → UNLOAD cargo → STORE it → SELL / DELIVER it → MONEY → UPGRADE → BIGGER SHIPS come
The best part: from doing it by hand to running a machine
At the start, you carry every box yourself, the same way you liked the 2 kg cart idea. Then:

Level	Unloading equipment	Feeling
1	Your hands (1 box at a time)	Hard work
2	Hand trolley (5 boxes)	Better
3	Forklift (whole pallets)	Fast
4	Small crane	Powerful
5	Container crane	A real port
6	Hired workers and automation	You're the boss now
By the end, you're standing in your office watching your port work without you. That's the payoff.

What you can unlock (small list on purpose)
🚢 Docks: wooden pier (small fishing boats) → concrete dock (cargo ships) → deep-water berth (giant container ships)

📦 Cargo types, unlocked one by one:

Fish: cheap, but spoils fast
Fruit: needs cold storage
Wood: heavy, needs a forklift
Cars: you drive them off the ship yourself
Containers: the big money, needs a crane
🏭 Storage: open ground → shed → warehouse → cold storage

👷 Workers: hire dock workers and drivers. They speed things up, but you pay their wages every day.

💰 Side businesses on the port (extra income):

Fuel station for boats
Ship repair yard
Sailors' restaurant
What keeps it from getting boring
Ship deadlines: every ship waits only a limited time. Unload before it leaves, or you pay a penalty.
Spoiling cargo: fish and fruit lose value every minute, so you have to hurry.
Changing prices: a market board shows today's prices. Store wood now and sell when the price goes up.
Reputation stars ⭐: do the job well and better ships and bigger contracts come to you.
Storm warnings: a 20-minute warning to secure cargo and tie down ships, or lose them. (Your favourite mechanic fits here too.)
Co-op (1–4 players)
One drives the forklift, one runs the crane, one handles orders and sales on a tablet.
Solo players can do everything, then hire workers.
Port stages (your progress)
🎣 Fishing pier: you, a few boxes, small boats
🏚️ Trade dock: forklift, warehouse, fruit and wood
🏗️ Cargo port: cranes, cars, workers
📦 Container port: big ships, big money
🌆 Mega port: the biggest on the coast
End goal: three options
A) Beat the rival 🏆 (my pick)
Across the bay is a rich rival port owner who laughs at your little pier. As you grow, you take his customers. Final goal: earn enough to buy his port. It's clear, satisfying, and easy to show as a goal in the game ("Rival's port: $5,000,000").

B) Pay back the loan 💸
You started by borrowing money. Pay back $1,000,000 within a set number of days or lose everything. Pressure from day one.

C) The giant ship 🚢
Final goal: your port is big enough for the world's largest container ship to choose it. One last huge unloading mission with everyone.

After the end goal, you can keep playing freely and keep growing.

Why it stays small
One port map that grows.
5 cargo types, about 15 upgrades, 5 stages.
First prototype: a wooden pier, one fishing boat, carrying boxes by hand, selling them, and buying a hand trolley. If carrying and selling feels good, the game works.
