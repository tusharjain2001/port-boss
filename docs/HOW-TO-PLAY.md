# Port Boss: overnight build (29 Oct 2026)

Double-click **PortBoss.exe**. It opens windowed (drag the edge to resize, or pick a window mode in Settings). Have Steam running for co-op; solo works without it.

## What to do
You run a run-down fishing pier. Boats come in with a horn, dock, and **Captain Mags** shouts a countdown: **two minutes**. Get the fish crates off the boat and walk up to **the buyer** (striped stall on the quay, left of the pier). Press **E** at the buyer: his panel lists the crates you're carrying (and the trolley's load if it's parked next to the stall) with his offer for each. **Fresh $15, okay $12, spoiled $4** (spoiled fish turns green and gets flies).
When the countdown runs out the boat **leaves with whatever is still aboard**. Three boats a day, about 8 minutes. At night you get the day's card and the **night shop**: the **hand trolley ($120)** holds 6 crates. Don't let go of it on the slipway.

Fell in? You **swim** (you never sink). Paddle to a **ladder** (pier sides, quay front) and press **E** to climb out, or swim up the slipway. While swimming, **E** tows one floating crate and **Q** lets it go.

## Controls (rebind in Settings > Controls)
| Keyboard / mouse | Gamepad | |
|---|---|---|
| WASD / mouse | left / right stick | move / look |
| Shift | LB | sprint (the stack wobbles more) |
| Space | A | jump |
| E or right click | X | grab a crate, stack more (max 5); sell at the buyer; tow / climb when swimming |
| Q | B | drop the top crate |
| R or left click | RT | throw the top crate |
| F | RB | push / let go of the trolley |
| H | | hide the controls panel |
| Esc | Start | pause (settings, invite friends, quit) |

Tips: a tall stack sways when you start, stop or turn. When it says **"Steady...!"**, slow down or steer back under it. Sprinting with 4–5 crates and turning hard will topple it. Mind the holes in the pier.

## Co-op (2–4 players, Steam)
- Host: main menu **Host co-op**, then Esc > **Invite Steam friends** (or friends open **Join a friend** and pick your port).
- Uses Valve's free test app 480 (Spacewar), so Steam may show you as playing "Spacewar".
- LAN / same PC: Join a friend > type the host IP > Connect.

## Settings
Graphics presets (Low/Medium/High/Ultra, auto-picked for your PC) plus individual options, display, audio (master, music, effects, ambience), controls (sensitivity, invert, key rebinding), gameplay (camera shake, shout text size, HUD). Everything applies immediately and is saved.

## Known issues
- **Co-op over Steam between two different accounts is untested** (one PC + one account can't test it: Steam won't connect an account to itself). Two copies on one PC through a real Steam lobby work. If friends can't connect over Steam, use LAN/IP.
- Art is a stylised blockout (primitives in a warm palette); the final character and art pass are still being decided.
- Music: only the night track so far (Suno, picked by Manan); day/rush/menu slots are silent until their tracks are chosen.
- Windows may ask for firewall access the first time you host co-op.
