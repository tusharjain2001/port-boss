# Port Boss: morning build (29 Oct 2026)

Double-click **PortBoss.exe**. It opens windowed (drag the edge to resize, or pick a window mode in Settings). Have Steam running for co-op; solo works without it.

Before your first game you make your **dock worker**: skin tone, face, hair, beard, headwear, top and colours (Randomise for a quick one). Change it any time from **Character** on the title menu or the pause menu; your mates see your look in co-op.

## The goal
Every game opens with the story (Space / Esc / click skips it): Grandpa's old fish pier is the last one on the coast that Marlowe doesn't own, and it's yours now.

You run the shabbiest berth on the coast: one concrete jetty, a tin shelter, an old crane, a crate store and a big walled yard to grow into; the fish market is across the road outside your gate. Out across the sea stands **Marlowe Port**, your rival's mega port: giant gantries, container terraces, and freighters calling all day (he'll make sure you hear about every one). **Earn enough to grow your berth.** The crew tin (top right) counts toward the next stage. At **$1500** the shop lets you **build the Concrete quay**: a proper shed and a forklift yard on your plot, and the boats bring **30% bigger catches**. After that the goal is the next stage (crane + warehouse).

## A day at the berth
Boats come in with a horn and dock at the end of the jetty. **Captain Mags** shouts a countdown: **two minutes**. Get the fish crates off the boat fast: put them on the **CRATE STORE** rack at the foot of the jetty (stand in front of it and press **Q**; hold **Q** to unload your whole stack). Then haul them to the **FISH MARKET**: out of the back gate, **across the road** (lorries go by both ways; they brake for you and lean on the horn, and no more than five ever queue up behind you) and up to the buyer's counter. A hand trolley saves a lot of trips: press **F** at its handle and you step in behind it; it goes where you go, stopping at walls, people and the water's edge. Press **E** at the buyer: his panel lists your crates with his offer for each, and **Sell all** sells the lot in one go. **Fresh $15, okay $12, spoiled $4** (spoiled fish goes green and gets flies). Every crate near you has a **freshness ring** over it with the buyer's price in the middle: it drains as the fish goes off and blinks just before the crate drops a grade (green fresh → amber okay → red spoiled), and red dots under it are knocks (each costs 25%). Sell the nearly-empty rings first. Fish on the rack keeps longer and the gulls can't reach it.
When the countdown runs out the boat **leaves with whatever is still aboard**. Three boats a day, about 8 minutes. On day 1 the **JOB** line (top left) and a yellow marker show you what to do next.
At night a small card sums up the day (click to skip): money earned, crates sold and lost, and **1–3 stars** for how much of the catch reached the market. Below it: the crew tin, tomorrow's captain, and the **Shop**, **Stats** (career stats, this run and all time; also in the pause menu) and **Start next day** buttons.

**The shop (Tab, any time; or Shop on the night card)** lists everything with its price, and things arrive right away, paid from the shared crew tin: the **hand trolley ($120)** holds 6 crates; the **bigger crate store ($250)** adds a third rack (18 crates); then the **cold store ($600)** adds a fourth rack (24) and a chiller, so fish on the racks stays fresh much longer; from day 3 a **forklift ($350)** with pallets; and the **Concrete quay ($1500)**. The bottom line shows what the market pays per crate.

**Stay off the boats when they sail:** anyone still aboard when a boat casts off gets thrown back onto the jetty by the captain (or over the side, if she's already out).

### The harbour fights back
- **Captains take turns:** **Mags** brings steady boats (2:00 at the jetty) on days 1–2, then **Bjorn** comes every other day (+50% crates, +25% pay, only 1:30). The morning shout says who's coming.
- **More boats:** from day 2 a second boat docks on the **left** side of the jetty while the first is still in.
- **Flying crates** knock people over (they drop their stack). Crates that land hard get **damaged**: each knock costs 25% of the price.
- **Your crew:** stand in front of a mate and press **F** to **shove** them; they drop whatever they're carrying. Use responsibly.
- **Gulls** go for crates nobody is watching: stand near your crates or lose fish.
- **Marlowe**, the rival, roars up to the end of your jetty in his speedboat every few minutes: a wall of spray soaks anyone standing there, then he stands in the boat laughing and pointing (speech bubble, cheeky horn tune) and soaks you again as he guns it away. His wake rocks the boat and slides loose crates. Now and then his **banner plane** drones over the yard with a new insult, and every time a freighter docks at Marlowe Port he sets off **fireworks**.
- **Weather** changes on its own, any time, any day: calm, breezy, overcast, rain, storm and back. Nothing warns you: read the sky and the sea. In rough weather the waves grow, the boat heaves, gulls clear off and gusts shove loose crates about. Get crates into the shelter or **tie them down (F)**.

Fell in? You **swim**, but only as long as your **stamina** lasts (the STAMINA bar at the bottom: about 25 s, less if you sprint; it refills on land). Boats are solid: one under way will shove you aside (or flat on your back on the quay). Paddle to a **ladder** (jetty sides, quay front) and press **E** to climb out, or swim up the slipway. Run out of stamina and the harbour master fishes you out onto the nearest ladder, dazed. While swimming, **E** tows one floating crate and **Q** lets it go.

## Controls (rebind in Settings > Controls)
| Keyboard / mouse | Gamepad | |
|---|---|---|
| WASD / mouse | left / right stick | move / look |
| Shift | LB | sprint (the stack wobbles more) |
| Space | A | jump (hold for a higher jump; a full stack is heavy) |
| E or right click | X | pick up a crate (from the ground, the rack, the trolley or a pallet), stack more (max 5; hold to keep picking up); sell at the buyer; drive the forklift; tow / climb when swimming |
| Q | B | put the top crate down: on the rack / in the trolley / on a pallet when you're next to one, else upright on the ground in front of you (hold to put them all down) |
| R or left click | RT | throw the top crate |
| F | RB | push / let go of the trolley; shove a crewmate in front of you; tie down / untie a crate |
| Tab | Back / View | open / close the shop |
| H | | hide the controls panel |
| Esc | Start | pause (settings, invite friends, quit) |

Tips: a tall stack sways when you start, stop or turn. When it says **"Steady...!"**, slow down or steer back under it. Sprinting with 4–5 crates and turning hard will topple it.

## Co-op (2–4 players)
- Host: main menu **Host co-op**, then Esc > **Invite friends** (or friends open **Join a friend** and pick your port).
- Uses Valve's free test app 480 (Spacewar), so Steam may show you as playing "Spacewar".
- LAN / same PC: Join a friend > type the host IP > Connect.

## Settings
Graphics presets (Low/Medium/High/Ultra, High by default) plus individual options, display, audio (master, music, effects, ambience), controls (sensitivity, invert, key rebinding), gameplay (camera shake, shout text size, HUD). Everything applies immediately and is saved.

## Known issues
- **Steam co-op needs testing with a friend.** One PC with one Steam account can't test it (Steam won't connect an account to itself). Two copies on one PC through a real Steam lobby work, and LAN/IP co-op is tested. If friends can't connect over Steam, use LAN/IP.
- Music: only the night track so far (picked by Manan); the day, rush and menu slots are silent until their tracks are chosen.
- The gull is still our own simple model (the art pack has no birds).
- Stages 1 and 2 are playable; stage 3 onwards (crane + warehouse, container berth, mega port) are goals on the HUD, not built yet.
- Windows may ask for firewall access the first time you host co-op.
